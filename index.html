4. Reklamlar: Google AdMob

Ödüllü, geçiş (interstitial) ve uygulama açılış reklamları için Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited tarafından sağlanan AdMob'u ve Google'ın kullanıcı mesajlaşma platformunu (UMP) kullanırız. Reklam SDK'sı, reklam göstermek, reklam performansını ölçmek, sahtekârlığı önlemek ve izin verdiyseniz reklamları kişiselleştirmek için şu verileri toplayabilir:

  • Reklam kimliği (Android Advertising ID; iOS'ta yalnızca izin verirseniz IDFA) ve uygulama grubu kimliği (App Set ID)
  • IP adresi ve bundan çıkarılan yaklaşık konum (ülke/şehir düzeyi)
  • Cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri (model, işletim sistemi, dil, uygulama sürümü)
  • Reklam etkileşimleri (gösterim, tıklama, izleme süresi) ve SDK tanılama/çökme verileri

Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre'de ilk açılışta Google'ın onay formu gösterilir (IAB TCF uyumlu). ABD'nin gizlilik yasası olan eyaletlerinde kişiselleştirilmiş reklamdan çıkma seçeneği sunulur. Tercihinizi sonradan Ayarlar › Gizlilik › Gizlilik seçenekleri ile değiştirebilirsiniz. Ayrıca Android'de Ayarlar › Google › Reklamlar bölümünden reklam kimliğinizi silebilir veya sıfırlayabilirsiniz. iOS'ta reklam izni Apple'ın "Uygulama İzleme Şeffaflığı" penceresiyle sorulur. İzin vermezseniz yine reklam görebilirsiniz, ancak bu reklamlar kişiselleştirilmez.

Google'ın verileri nasıl kullandığı: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Gizlilik politikası: policies.google.com/privacy

İleride "Paycheck Pro" sunulursa, sahipleri ara reklam ve açılış reklamı görmez.

5. Satın alımlar: Google Play, Apple ve RevenueCat

Şu anki sürümde uygulama içi satın alma bulunmaz ve RevenueCat'e hiçbir veri gönderilmez. Bu bölüm, ileride satın alma eklenirse geçerli olacaktır.

"Paycheck Pro" (reklamları kaldırma / abonelik) ödemeleri tamamen Google Play Faturalandırma (iOS'ta Apple App Store) tarafından işlenir. Ödeme yönteminiz ve fatura bilgileriniz onlarda kalır, bize iletilmez. Satın alımınızı doğrulamak ve cihazlarınız arasında geri yüklemek için RevenueCat, Inc. (ABD) hizmetini kullanırız. RevenueCat'e şunlar iletilir:

  • Rastgele oluşturulan anonim bir uygulama kullanıcı kimliği (adınız veya e-postanız değil)
  • Satın alma makbuzu/jetonu, ürün, tarih, fiyat, para birimi ve mağaza ülkesi
  • Cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri (işletim sistemi, uygulama sürümü) ve IP adresi

RevenueCat gizlilik politikası: revenuecat.com/privacy · Google: policies.google.com/privacy · Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the Paycheck to Freedom mobile game (Android package com.omerbs4.paychecktofreedom) and its web version (the "Game") process, why, and what your rights are. The Game is a personal-finance simulation: its companies, stocks and prices are fictional, no real money is traded, and nothing in it is investment advice.

2. In short

  • No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or camera.
  • Your save, settings and records are stored only on your device. We run no server and cannot see them.
  • The Game is free and shows Google AdMob ads. Our ad partner may collect data such as device identifiers. Personalised ads require your consent, which you can change at any time under Settings › Privacy › Privacy options.
  • The current version has no in-app purchases. If an optional "Paycheck Pro" purchase is added later, it will be handled by Google Play (Apple on iOS) and verified through RevenueCat. We never see your card details.

4. Advertising: Google AdMob

We use AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, for rewarded, interstitial and app-open ads. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if you consent, personalise ads, the ad SDK may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only with your permission) and App Set ID
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/city level)
  • Device and app information (model, OS, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, view time) and SDK diagnostics/crash data

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (IAB TCF) is shown on first launch. In US states with privacy laws, an opt-out of personalised advertising is offered. You can change your choice later under Settings › Privacy › Privacy options. On Android you can also delete or reset your advertising ID under Settings › Google › Ads. On iOS, permission is requested through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

If "Paycheck Pro" is offered later, its owners will see no interstitial or app-open ads.

5. Purchases: Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat

The current version has no in-app purchases and sends no data to RevenueCat. This section applies if purchases are added in the future.

Payments for "Paycheck Pro" (ad removal / subscription) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing (the Apple App Store on iOS). Your payment method and billing details stay with them and are not passed to us. We use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) to validate purchases and restore them across your devices. RevenueCat receives:

  • A randomly generated anonymous app user ID (not your name or email)
  • The purchase receipt/token, product, date, price, currency and store country
  • Device and app information (OS, app version) and IP address

RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy · Google: policies.google.com/privacy · Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy

6. Notifications

If you turn on reminders (Settings › Reminders), reminders such as "your missions are waiting" are scheduled locally on your device. We have no notification server and collect no push token. You can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

7. Sharing

When you tap "Share" or "Challenge", your result text, a challenge code containing your fictional character's details, or an image of the result card is handed to your device's share sheet. You choose what to share and with whom. This content is not sent to us.

8. Web version

The web version has no ads and no purchases. Game data stays in your browser's local storage. The site is hosted on Vercel Inc. infrastructure. The host may keep standard server logs (including IP address and browser information) to deliver and secure the site. We use no analytics or tracking cookies.

9. What we do not use

The Game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo library or SMS permissions. We operate no analytics tool, account system or server of our own. We do not sell your personal data for money.

10. Legal bases (GDPR / KVKK)

  • Consent: personalised advertising and device access for advertising.
  • Legitimate interests: serving non-personalised ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and security.
  • Performance of a contract: once purchases are offered, providing and restoring the Pro features you bought.
  • Legal obligation: tax and accounting records kept by the stores and payment providers.

11. Retention

  • On-device data: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data: under Google's retention policies (see policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  • Purchase records: for as long as your subscription is active and the purchase must remain restorable, plus any statutory retention period.

12. International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data in other countries, including the USA. These transfers rely on the providers' safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For users in Türkiye, transfers abroad rely on your explicit consent and/or the providers' standard contracts under Article 9 of the KVKK (Law No. 6698). Without ad consent, no transfer for personalisation takes place.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK Art. 11, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws), you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g. the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority, or your EU country's data protection authority).

  • On-device data: you control it directly. Delete it under Settings › Data, or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data: withdraw consent under Settings › Privacy › Privacy options. For data held by Google, use Google's tools.
  • Purchase data: email hypeheroic@gmail.com. Including your Google Play order number (GPA.…) is enough for us to find your anonymous ID.

We reply to requests within 30 days at the latest.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, our ad partner's processing of device identifiers and interaction data for personalised advertising may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws. To opt out, use Settings › Privacy › Privacy options, or limit ad tracking in your device's advertising-ID settings. You will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

15. Children

The Game is designed for adults (18+) and is not directed at children under 13 (or, in the EU, under your country's age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children of that age. If you become aware of such a case, contact us and we will delete the data.

16. Security

All communication with service providers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). On-device game data is kept inside the operating system's app sandbox.
